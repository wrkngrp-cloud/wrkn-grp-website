# Cowork Prompt — WRKN GRP World-Class Brand Identity System

---

You are a world-class brand identity director and design engineer working for **WRKN GRP** — a strategic creative partner for challenger brands building in Africa. Your job is to design and document a **complete, world-class brand identity system** for WRKN GRP itself: the verbal identity, the visual identity, the motion and material language, and the application rules, delivered as a coherent, reference-grade brand guidelines system plus the supporting asset boards.

This is not a logo refresh and not a website. It is the full identity system the website was one expression of. Treat the website we already built as proof of the territory, not the ceiling. Everything below is locked brand truth unless I explicitly change it. Do not invent facts about the company, do not add scope from the exclusions list, and do not soften the craft standard.

## 1. Who WRKN GRP is (locked)

- **Positioning line:** *Led by Strategy. Built in Culture.*
- **What we are:** the strategic creative partner for challenger brands building in Africa. We help founders and CMOs move from having a product to having a point of view — from being seen, to being remembered.
- **The unique selling point (the thing to build the whole system around):** **cultural intelligence.** Strategy is a core strength, but it is table stakes. What makes WRKN GRP irreplaceable is establishing **cultural resonance and relevance**, and engineering **mental associations and recall** — making a brand feel familiar, relevant, and first-to-mind. The system must dramatize *harmony*: every part of a brand tuned to the same cultural key so the pieces resonate instead of compete. Never reduce the story to "strategy first, execution follows" — that is shallow and already assumed.
- **Footprint:** Lagos. Nairobi. London. Toronto. Dubai. Lagos-native sensibility is the anchor.
- **Real clients (the only ones that exist):** Nyla (digital bank) and the Aliko Dangote Foundation. Do not invent clients, logos, stats, or testimonials.
- **The service ladder (five rungs, each leads to the next):** 01 Brand Positioning Workshop → 02 Brand Strategy Sprint → 03 Creative Advisory Retainer → 04 Campaign Strategy & Brand Initiatives → 05 Fractional CMO.
- **Only social channel:** Instagram, @wrkngrp.

## 2. The deliverable

Produce a **WRKN GRP Brand Identity System v1** as a reference library, not a single dump. Author it as self-contained, theme-aware HTML brand boards (publishable as artifacts) plus a written guidelines document. It must cover, each section authoritative on its territory:

1. **Brand foundation** — positioning, the cultural-intelligence thesis, brand story, values, the single differentiator, and the long-term proprietary asset (the accumulating cultural-recall method WRKN GRP builds with every engagement). A named audience in context (a challenger-brand founder and a CMO in an African market), not a demographic.
2. **Verbal identity** — voice and tone, messaging architecture, the tagline system and how sub-lines ladder off *Led by Strategy. Built in Culture.*, naming conventions, and worked copy examples for real touchpoints (deck cover, proposal intro, pitch email, Instagram caption, case-study opener, out-of-office one-liner).
3. **Logo system** — the wordmark (serif-italic "wrkn" set against bold sans "grp." with the full stop as a fixed anchor), its construction and clear-space, the monogram/modular **W**, lockups, sizing floors, one-color and reversed use, and misuse.
4. **Color** — the locked three-color palette below, with roles, ratios, accessible pairings (state contrast ratios), tints for UI states, and the rule that gold is a scalpel, never a highlighter.
5. **Typography** — display and body families below, the type scale, weights, the italic-for-emphasis rule, numerals as an editorial device, and worked hierarchy specimens.
6. **Material & texture language** — the "ceramic" system: warm, lit, bevel-edged ceramic forms (the modular bricks and ring rungs), plus grain, the dot-matrix field, and hairline/wayfinding rules. This is the tactile signature; specify it as carefully as color.
7. **Motion principles** — how the brand moves (see §5), stated as durable principles with easing, timing, and "motion must carry meaning" as law, not decoration.
8. **Art direction & imagery** — how photography and client work are shown (angled/in-hand mockups over flat scans; real work only), crop specs, and the treatment that keeps everything in the warm-black / cream / gold world.
9. **Iconography & graphic devices** — numerals (01–05), wayfinding bars, chapter numerals, progress hairlines, the modular-W grid.
10. **Application** — the system on a business card, letterhead, deck template, proposal cover, social templates, email signature, and a case-study layout, each shown, not described.
11. **Usage law** — a do / don't board, and the exclusions in §7 written as guardrails.

Close with a one-page **decision log**: what was deliberately left out and why, so no one helpfully adds it back.

## 3. Visual identity — locked tokens

**Palette (only these three; no other accent colors ever):**
- Warm black `#0A0A0A`
- Cream `#F4EFE6`
- Gold `#EFC835`

Neutrals are drawn from warm-black and cream at reduced opacity, never a cold grey. Gold is reserved for the single most important thing in any composition (the active state, the "one true thing," the emphasized word). If gold is fighting the layout, reduce its saturation or footprint — do not add a fourth color.

**Typography:**
- **Display:** *Balkist* (licensed; when unavailable, *Sentient* from Fontshare is the live fallback, then a serif system stack). Headings are set **upright** by default. **Italic is reserved for emphasis inside a title** (one or two words), never as the general heading style.
- **Body:** *General Sans* (with *Heuvel Grotesk* to slot in when licensed).
- Numerals are an editorial device — large, spaced, sometimes italic serif as a chapter marker. Uppercase labels carry generous letter-spacing. Keep running text near 65 characters wide; set a scale and stay on it; give headings balanced wrapping.

**Logo:** serif-italic "wrkn" + bold sans "grp." + a fixed period. It must read in a single `currentColor`, so it sits on warm black or cream with no separate asset. The modular **W** monogram is the reducible mark and the atom of the assembly system.

## 4. Verbal identity — locked rules

- **Voice:** sharp, Lagos-native, evidence-backed, confident without hype. Write from the reader's side of the screen.
- **Copy law:** no em dashes as a stylistic crutch (use them only for true parenthetical breaks, sparingly). **Banned words:** delve, robust, landscape, pivotal, "In conclusion," and the rest of the AI-tell vocabulary. No inflated symbolism, no rule-of-three padding, no vague attributions. Specific beats clever.
- **Messaging spine:** problem (a brand is not its scattered pieces — individually impressive, collectively forgettable) → mechanism (cultural intelligence tunes every part to the same key: resonance and relevance) → payoff (one thing, everywhere, remembered — mental availability by design). Every headline should map to one of those three beats.

## 5. Proven creative territory (carry these forward as brand-level ideas)

These concepts were built, tested in-browser, and validated on the website. They are the brand's signature moves; the identity system should codify them, not discard them.

- **The assembly / modular W.** Thirteen labeled ceramic bricks — each a piece of execution (Identity, Motion, Social, Campaign, Print, Editorial, Decks, Digital, Type, Events, Voice, Illustration, Campaign) — begin scattered in space and travel into the modular **W**. Every piece stays face-out so its label reads; then, as the single gold brick — **the "One True Thing"** — lands, the execution pieces flip to their blank backs, leaving the one true thing as the only text standing. The argument made physical: **the parts are just noise until cultural intelligence tunes them into one resonant brand.** Codify "One True Thing" as a core brand concept: the single organizing idea every engagement is built around.
- **Harmony / "the same key."** The mechanism beat is *"Everything in the same key"* — cultural intelligence as the tuning that makes the pieces resonate instead of compete. Music/harmony is the sanctioned metaphor for how WRKN GRP unifies a brand. Use it; it is richer and truer than "strategy then execution."
- **The ladder.** The five services are literal rungs of a ceramic ring-ladder you climb (01→05); the active rung centers, turns gold, pulls forward, glows, while the others recede into a cream haze. "Every product leads to the next." Keep it as a premium, legible climb — distinct rungs on visible rails with real depth — never an overlapping chain.
- **Strategy draws the line; culture builds the field.** The site's intro makes the tagline physical: a single gold line draws outward (Led by Strategy), a field of ceramic bricks blooms from it (Built in Culture), then detonates outward into the page. This "line → field → world" motion is the brand's origin gesture; specify it as a reusable identity animation.
- **Material signature.** Warm, lit, bevel-edged **ceramic** — the same material language across bricks, rings, and mark. Editorial, kinetic, print-minded. Plus a cursor-reactive **dot-matrix field** and fine grain for tactility.

## 6. Craft & quality standard (non-negotiable)

- **World-class means anti-generic.** No templated "AI-agency" defaults: no cream-plus-terracotta cliché, no lone acid-green pop, no purple-blue gradient hero, no Inter/Space-Grotesk as the safe face, no emoji section markers, no everything-centered, no rounded-card-with-accent-bar. Where this brief pins a direction, follow it exactly; where it doesn't, do not spend the freedom on a default.
- **Every device must encode something true.** Numbering, eyebrows, dividers, and the modular grid carry meaning (a real sequence, a real hierarchy), never decoration.
- **Motion carries meaning or it is cut.** An orchestrated moment beats scattered effects. Specify easing (expressive `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for entrances), durations, and restraint. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with static/instant fallbacks that never remove content.
- **Accessibility is part of "premium":** legible contrast on both warm-black and cream grounds, visible focus states, real alt text, theme-aware color tokens.
- **Design both grounds.** The system lives on warm black *and* on cream; every color, type, and component is specified for both, not inverted naively.

## 7. Locked exclusions (guardrails — do not add these back)

Do not include, invent, or imply: pricing; team bios, headshots, or a founder name; testimonials or quotes; Sweetness Studios or any other venture; an internal roadmap or planning content; any social channel other than Instagram @wrkngrp; fabricated clients, logos, awards, or statistics; a fourth brand color; a second display face used as a crutch; italic as a default heading style. If any of these seems helpful, it is scope drift — log it in the decision log instead of adding it.

## 8. How to work

1. **Research the real market first.** Study how the best strategic/cultural agency brands present themselves (verbal and visual), and how challenger African brands read — then build the system from WRKN GRP's own truth, not from a generic best-practice template. Local reality overrides general best practice.
2. **Propose the spine before the depth.** Draft the foundation (positioning + cultural-intelligence thesis + one differentiator) first and get it right; everything else hangs off it.
3. **Build a complete first draft, then audit it** against §6 and §7 before polishing. A complete draft with visible issues beats a partial one.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Every rule earns a rendered specimen (a real card, a real deck cover, a real caption), not a description of one.
5. **Version and date the system, and end with next actions** and the decision log of exclusions.

## Definition of done

A brand identity system a top studio would sign: foundation, verbal, logo, color, type, material, motion, art direction, iconography, application, and usage — each authoritative, each shown with real WRKN GRP content, all inside the warm-black / cream / gold ceramic world, honoring every locked rule and every exclusion, and codifying the four signature moves (the modular-W assembly and "One True Thing," harmony / "the same key," the ladder, and "line → field → world"). Cultural intelligence — resonance, relevance, recall — is the thesis the whole system argues.
